About the Game

Studio Tycoon is a strategy sim that lets you build your dream film studio from the ground up. Hire from a pool of over 2,000 unique characters including actors, directors, writers, and crew. You'll manage films, develop rivalries, make deals, chase awards, and unleash gossip across the industry. The game is in very early development, but this class has been helping me move things forward. Hoping to release next year!
